# Phytotherapeutic-Nano-Approaches-in-Management-of-Huntington-s-Disease-
Overview
Huntington’s Disease (HD) is a progressive inherited neurodegenerative disorder characterized by motor dysfunction, cognitive decline, and psychiatric disturbances. Current treatments mainly provide symptomatic relief, with no definitive cure available.
This review focuses on:
Pathogenesis and genetic basis of HD
Epidemiology and risk factors
Clinical symptoms and diagnostic approaches
AI and machine learning in HD diagnosis
Phytotherapeutic compounds with neuroprotective potential
Nano-drug delivery systems for enhanced brain targeting
Emerging treatment strategies and future prospects
Key Highlights
Discussion on mutant Huntingtin protein (mHTT) and CAG repeat expansion
Role of oxidative stress, mitochondrial dysfunction, neuroinflammation, and protein aggregation in HD progression
Application of nanotechnology to improve blood-brain barrier (BBB) penetration
Use of medicinal plant extracts and phytochemicals as neuroprotective agents
AI/ML-based diagnostic approaches for early detection and disease prediction
Advanced therapeutic approaches including gene therapy, siRNA delivery, and nanoparticle systems

Objective
The primary objective of this review is to evaluate the therapeutic potential of phytotherapeutic nano-approaches for improving neuroprotection, drug bioavailability, targeted CNS delivery, and overall treatment outcomes in Huntington’s Disease.
Future Scope
Future research may focus on:
Personalized medicine approaches
AI-integrated diagnostics
Gene editing technologies like CRISPR-Cas9
Advanced nano-delivery systems
Combination therapies targeting multiple disease pathways
